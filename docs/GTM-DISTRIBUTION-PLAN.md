# Aquilla — Distribution & Marketing Plan

**The autopilot trust-manufacturing engine**

_Author: strategy synthesis, 2026-06-25. Working back from ICP → trust → brand
associations → distribution → the near-autopilot content engine fed by daily
meeting transcripts._

---

## 0. The one idea that makes this plan different

You are not selling into a market where trust is **bought**. You are selling
into a market where trust is **conferred** — by credentialed consultants, by
standards bodies, by foundation alliances. In this world a new tool is not
adopted because it markets well; it is adopted because the right people have
been seen to vouch for it.

So the job of marketing here is **not lead-gen first**. The job is
**manufacturing legible trust at scale, then using LinkedIn as the distribution
layer for that trust.** This inverts normal SaaS marketing — and it happens to
be the thing you're uniquely good at, because:

> **Your product and your marketing engine are the same architecture.**
> The product is an AI system that turns messy human input (a translation
> session) into structured, trustworthy, reviewable output. The marketing
> engine is an AI system that turns messy human input (your daily meeting
> transcripts) into structured, trustworthy, reviewable published content. You
> dogfood your own agent infrastructure to run your own go-to-market. That is
> both the autopilot mechanism **and** a marketing proof point no competitor can
> copy.

Everything below is built on that.

## 1. ICP — two engines, never averaged

The single biggest framing error is to make **translation consultants** the
market. They aren't. There are only a few hundred credentialed consultants on
earth — too few to be "the future." They are the **trust ballast**: tiny,
high-leverage, reached one-to-one, and their entire job in this plan is to make
the movement *safe to adopt*. They are a means (credibility), not the end
(scale). The moment they become the center of gravity, you've built a tool for a
shrinking priesthood.

**The actual market is the democratization of translation — and the web app is
what unlocks it.** Paratext is Windows-only desktop; it is *structurally
incapable* of serving the mass demographic. A browser app runs on a Chromebook
in Nairobi and a phone in Jakarta. Moving to the web didn't just improve UX — it
**changed who is allowed to participate in Bible translation.** That is the
headline of the company.

> The Reformation democratized scripture *reading* — "the priesthood of all
> believers." Aquilla democratizes scripture *translation*: **translation by all
> believers, with the experts keeping it trustworthy.** The slogan already
> encodes the balance — *Scale* (the movement) *without losing trust* (the
> ballast). Don't over-rotate on the second clause.

### 1a. The two engines

| | **Engine 1 — Trust** | **Engine 2 — Movement** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Consultants, standards bodies (SIL/UBS), ETEN, funders/foundations | MTTs, church/denomination leaders, project owners & PMs, community reviewers, Global South, emerging/student translators |
| Size | Tiny, high-leverage | Large — the real growth |
| Motion | 1:1, endorsement, conferences, high-touch | Product-led + owned media at scale, near-autopilot |
| Channel | **LinkedIn** + conferences + direct | **YouTube** + in-product PLG + community |
| Narrative | rigor · fidelity · no shortcuts | anyone can join the work — here's how |
| Job | make it *safe* to adopt | make it *spread* |

**Do not blend the two narratives.** "Democratize/disrupt" energy in front of
consultants reads as a MAST-style threat; "rigorous consultant gatekeeping" in
front of eager MTTs reads as exclusion. Same company, two stories, matched to
channel. Segment, don't average.

### 1b. Cohort fracture (informs pricing)

| Cohort | Who | Buys on | Pricing reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Funded establishment** | SIL affiliates, UBS national Bible societies, Seed Company / Pioneer / FCBH, ETEN-funded programs | trust, reliability, Paratext fidelity | can pay; expects rigor, security, references |
| **B — Church-led / Global South** | unfoldingWord/CCBT-style mother-tongue teams, local-church projects | accessibility, free-or-near-free, low friction | must be free in the field |

> The defensible model: **free-for-the-field, pay-for-the-oversight-layer.** The
> manager/owner Overview, org administration, audit, and audio QA are where
> Cohort A's money is — exactly the features your owner/PM personas
> (Wendi/Randall/Anna) already consume. Charging both cohorts the same way fails
> with one of them (the Pushpay vs Tithe.ly lesson).

### 1c. The audiences, mapped to who reaches them

- **MTTs / bilingual authors & validators** — live in the editor; the volume of
  the movement. Reached by **the product itself + YouTube tutorials + community.**
- **Owners / program managers / project leads** — *don't read the target
  language*; consume trust/quality signals (health, validation, audit,
  progress). The commercial anchor. Reached by **LinkedIn + case studies.**
- **Translation consultants** — run the formal "consultant check"; accredited via
  SIL/UBS (FOBAI). The credibility ballast (the Clio bar-association play: one
  endorser > fifty demos). Reached by **1:1 + conferences**, never a mass channel.
- **Funders / foundations** — fuel, not users. Reached by **LinkedIn thought
  leadership + direct.**

## 2. The wedge — "coexist before you replace"

Do **not** position as the Paratext killer. The two best analogues both won by
coexisting first:

- **Scripture Forge** (SIL's own web app) earned adoption by making **Paratext
  the auth provider** and syncing back via Paratext Notes — not by competing.
  ~500 projects, and Paratext telemetry shows AI-draft teams completed ~1,000
  more verses/project/year. They proved the web layer is additive.
- **Esri ArcMap → ArcGIS** ran a **14-year parallel-working window**. Forcing
  power users off muscle-memory tools fails; letting them keep their world while
  gaining new powers is the wedge.

**Aquilla's wedge message:** *"Aquilla is the collaborative browser layer on top
of your Paratext world. Your USFM round-trips cleanly. Your consultants keep
their checks. Your managers finally get to see what's happening — in any
language."*

USFM/Paratext/Scripture-Burrito round-trip fidelity is therefore not a feature
bullet. **It is the headline of the brand.** It is the thing that makes you safe
to try.

## 3. The trust graph — associations we must manufacture

Trust in this market is a **graph of endorsements and standards memberships**.
We build it node by node, and every node becomes content. Priority order:

**Tier 1 — credibility-defining (pursue immediately)**
1. **Formal ETEN Innovation Lab project listing.** You already have a beachhead
   — ETEN has named Codex/Aquilla in its ecosystem. Convert that into an
   explicit listing. This is the single highest-leverage trust node: it signals
   theological neutrality + mission alignment to the entire consultant community.
2. **3–5 named senior-consultant design partners**, on the record. The Clio bar-
   association move. Their quotes and case studies become your most-shared
   content.
3. **Open-standards conformance, stated publicly**: USFM round-trip, Scripture
   Burrito, Digital Bible Library submission path, ProgressBible partner listing.

**Tier 2 — table-stakes trust (becoming mandatory, per Gloo's market signal)**
4. **Data-sovereignty posture, explicit and prominent**: CARE principles
   (Collective benefit, Authority to control, Responsibility, Ethics), data
   residency, and a hard public commitment — *"we never train on community
   content without consent."* Critical for restricted-access countries and
   minority-language groups. Publish a security/sovereignty page (the clinical-
   trials "21 CFR Part 11" lesson: turn trust into a verifiable credential, not a
   sales claim). Put SOC 2 on the roadmap.

**Tier 3 — ecosystem presence**
5. **Conference presence**, in priority order: **ICCM** (International Conference
   on Computing & Mission — the watering hole; regional editions Americas/East
   Africa/West Africa/Europe), then Missio Nexus, ICOM. Workshops > booths in
   expert communities.
6. **LingTranSoft.info** listing (the SIL software wiki TCs actually consult)
   and the tools.bible / paratext.org "related software" directories.

**Associations to AVOID** (these are trust-negatives in SIL/UBS circles):
- Any visual/verbal association with **Wycliffe Associates / MAST** — the speed
  controversy is a lasting scar.
- **"AI replaces the translator."** Frame AI as *Draft Zero* requiring human
  refinement — the same frame Scripture Forge uses.
- **Any "we're faster" claim without quality evidence.** Speed-without-rigor is
  the exact thing this community punishes.

## 5. The autopilot content engine (the 99%)

This is the machine that turns your **daily meeting transcripts and insights**
into a steady, trustworthy, multi-channel content stream — with you and your
team injecting raw material and approving, nothing more.

### 5a. Architecture — build the brain, buy the plumbing

You already build AI agents on Cloudflare Workers + Neon + R2 + Queues + Durable
Objects. **The extraction/voice/approval brain is worth owning** (it's your
proprietary trust layer and a dogfooding proof point). **The transcription and
publishing plumbing is worth buying** (don't fight LinkedIn's API).

```
┌─ INGEST (buy) ────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Fireflies / Granola / Otter  →  webhook  →  R2 transcript store │
│ + your own daily "insights" notes dropped into the same bucket  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                          │
┌─ TRIAGE  (build · Cloudflare Worker + Claude Haiku) ───────────┐
│ Classify every segment:                                        │
│  narrative_anecdote | product_claim | customer_pain |          │
│  build_in_public | internal_only | discard                     │
│ HARD GATE: flag customer names, financials, unreleased         │
│  features, restricted-country/minority-language detail,        │
│  competitive intel  →  safe_to_publish=false  →  HOLD table.   │
│ Drafts are NEVER generated from held segments.                 │
│ (Run a keyword blocklist in parallel with the LLM classifier.) │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                          │  (only safe segments continue)
┌─ EXTRACT  (build · Worker + Claude Sonnet) ───────────────────┐
│ Per category → "insight atoms" in a Neon `insight_atoms` table │
│  · narratives (problem→attempt→insight, founder's exact words) │
│  · product claims (+ evidence basis + verified/inferred flag)  │
│  · pain quotes (verbatim)                                      │
│  · build-in-public moments                                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                          │
┌─ DRAFT  (build · one Worker per format) ──────────────────────┐
│ Inject atoms + VOICE LIBRARY (few-shot {flat → branded} pairs  │
│  in a `voice_examples` Neon table) + format rules:             │
│  · LinkedIn text post   · LinkedIn carousel (8–12 slides)      │
│  · native video script  · newsletter section · case-study para │
│ ReviewerAgent scores the HOOK 1–5; only 4+ surfaces to humans. │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                          │
┌─ APPROVE  (build · Queue → Durable Object holds paused state) ─┐
│ Daily digest to Slack/email: each draft = Approve / Edit /     │
│  Discard. Approve-by-emoji (✅) fires publish. Cap 3–5/day.    │
│ The DO is your LangGraph-style interrupt(): state parks until  │
│  a human resume signal arrives.                                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                          │  (approved only)
┌─ PUBLISH (hybrid — see §6c) ───────────────────────────────────┐
│ YouTube   → DIRECT YouTube Data API from your stack (DO/queue)  │
│   — uploads + captions + scheduling are fully automatable.     │
│ LinkedIn/X→ Postiz cloud hub (API + MCP/agent CLI); native      │
│   LinkedIn video cuts, NOT YouTube links. Fallback: Ayrshare.  │
│ Newsletter→ Beehiiv create-post API (human clicks Send = 1 tap).│
│ Blog/SEO  → Ghost Admin API.                                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Your unfair asset:** you already have the **Showcase recording harness**
(`e2e/recordings/`, cursor + zoom + captions) that produces real point-and-click
product videos on autopilot. Wire it into the DRAFT stage so product-walkthrough
clips are generated from a script with **zero filming**. This is what makes the
**YouTube** engine (§6) possible at cadence — one master recording fans out into
a YouTube long-form upload, a native LinkedIn cut, and a vertical Short. Almost
no competitor in this space can produce credible native-video product demos at
content cadence.

### 5b. Why the confidentiality gate is non-negotiable here

This is not generic content safety. Your transcripts will discuss
**restricted-access countries, minority-language communities, and named
partners.** A leak isn't a PR problem — it's a *physical-safety and trust*
problem that would destroy your standing with the exact consultants you need.
The triage HOLD gate is therefore both brand-safety **and** a live demonstration
of the sovereignty values you're selling. Make it bulletproof: LLM classifier +
keyword blocklist + human review of every held segment before it's ever re-fed.

### 5c. Build-vs-buy summary

| Stage | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Transcription | **Buy** (Fireflies/Granola) | Solved; don't build |
| Triage / redaction | **Build** | Proprietary trust layer; safety-critical |
| Extraction + voice | **Build** | Your moat; dogfoods your own agents |
| Approval queue | **Build** (Queue + DO) | You already have the infra; 1–2 days |
| YouTube publish | **Build-direct** (Data API) | Generous quota (~100 units/upload); own the harness→upload path |
| LinkedIn / X publish | **Buy** (Postiz cloud; fallback Ayrshare) | LinkedIn personal-posting approval is the hard part; a hub absorbs it |
| Newsletter | **Buy** (Beehiiv) | Create-post API + 1-tap send |
| Blog/SEO | **Buy** (Ghost API) | Cleanest dev-controlled CMS |

Steady-state human cost: **~1–2 hrs/week** (prompt tuning + approvals).
LLM cost at 20–30 pieces/week: **~$20–60/month.** Negligible.

## 6. Distribution — channel architecture

Two co-primary channels, one for each engine, fed by the same pipeline.

| Channel | Engine | Reaches | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Movement | MTTs, church teams, coordinators, Global South, students | Search-first + evergreen + dubbable; the bigger long-term growth engine |
| **LinkedIn** (founder-led) | Trust | owners/PMs, funders, consultants | Personal reach + professional/funder layer |
| Newsletter | both | warm audience | owned, cross-cohort |
| In-product PLG | Movement | everyone who lands | free-for-the-field; onboarding videos embedded |
| Community (Telegram/WhatsApp/Discord) | Movement | MTTs, reviewers | Global South lives on mobile chat, not feeds |

### 6a. YouTube — the movement engine (co-primary)

YouTube is a **search engine**, and your niche is nearly empty: almost no video
competes for "how to import USFM," "back-translation workflow," "mother-tongue
translation software." First-mover on those long-tail queries is durable and
surfaces in Google + AI Overviews too.

- **The open lane:** BibleProject is the gold standard (55+ per-language
  channels, 60% of views international) — but they do *theology*. Wycliffe / SIL
  / unfoldingWord run YouTube as dead media libraries. **Nobody does
  BibleProject-quality video for the tools-and-process layer.** That's the lane.
- **Track mix ~70/30**: evergreen tutorial (ranks, reduces support load, drives
  PLG signups) vs vision/movement/testimony (lands once an audience exists).
- **Global South unlock:** multi-language caption tracks via `captions.insert`,
  plus **YouTube Aloud auto-dubbing** (27 languages incl. Hindi, Portuguese,
  Indonesian, French as of Feb 2026; >25% of watch time from non-primary-language
  viewers). On-brand for a minority-language mission; almost no competitor uses
  it. (Aloud is enabled per-video in Studio — not API-triggerable.)
- **Shorts = discovery funnel only.** Cut a 45–90s hook from a long video with a
  hard end-card CTA to the full tutorial/signup. High Shorts views with zero
  signups is the B2B vanity trap — don't chase them standalone.
- **SEO hygiene:** keyword in first 30 chars of title and first 15 sec of speech;
  chapters in every description (they surface as Google "key moments"); playlists
  by workflow stage; a 60–90s channel trailer aimed at coordinators.
- **One recording → four artifacts:** YouTube long-form · native LinkedIn cut ·
  vertical Short · thumbnail image-post. The Showcase harness makes the master.

### 6b. LinkedIn — the trust engine (co-primary)

**Founder-led.** Personal profiles get ~561% more reach and ~8× the engagement
of company pages on identical content; buyers trust people over brands ~3×. The
company page exists only as a due-diligence anchor.

**Recommended weekly rhythm (3–5 posts, from the atomized transcript stream):**

| Day | Format | Pillar it serves |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Text — a POV / contrarian take on translation tech | Expert at the center |
| Tue | Personal story (build-in-public, from a transcript) | Trust / humanity |
| Wed | **Carousel** (highest-engagement format: ~6.6%, ~4× text) — a framework, a checklist, "how a consultant check actually works" | Make quality visible |
| Thu | **Native video** (Showcase-generated walkthrough) | Compress coordination |
| Fri | Industry breakdown / standards explainer (USFM, Burrito, 2033 goals) | Preserve trust |

**Rules the engine must enforce (2025–26 algorithm reality):**
- First 60 minutes decide reach → coordinate team comments (substantive, not
  "great post"). Internal Slack ping when a post drops.
- **No external links in the post body** (~60% reach penalty) → link in first
  comment.
- Dwell time > likes; saves/shares are the highest-intent signals → write
  save-worthy carousels.
- No engagement-bait ("agree?") — penalized.
- 90 days minimum before judging traction; compounding over 6–12 months.

**Beyond posting:**
- **LinkedIn newsletter** — one atomized theme/week; subscriber notifications =
  guaranteed reach that compounds.
- **20 min/day human engagement** — substantive comments in the feeds of target
  consultants and non-competing peers. (This is the one piece that should stay
  human — it's relationship equity, and it's where DMs start.)
- **Warm DMs only** — triggered by a thoughtful comment or a target-account
  profile view. ~10% reply rate vs 5% cold email. Never mass-DM (instant ban
  risk).
- **Thought-Leader Ads** once an organic post clears ~8% engagement — promote it
  to a hand-built audience of the ~few-hundred-person ICP via Sales Navigator
  targeting. Start ~$1.5–2k/mo only after organic proves the message. At this
  ICP size, 200–300 *right* followers in real conversations is a win.

**Automation safety line:** automate drafting, scheduling (via official APIs),
and analytics. **Never** automate comments, DMs, connection requests, or
scraping — LinkedIn's 2025 enforcement wave was real (Taplio, Kleo, Apollo all
hit). The cookie-auth browser-extension model is the thing that gets accounts
banned. Official-API tools only.

### 6c. The publishing stack — a hybrid

| Surface | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | **Direct YouTube Data API** from your stack (Durable Object or queue handles the resumable chunked upload — Workers' 30s CPU limit can't) | `videos.insert` ~100 quota units (~100 uploads/day free), `captions.insert`, `status.publishAt` scheduling — fully automatable; own the harness→upload path |
| **LinkedIn + X + Shorts** | **Postiz cloud (Team, ~$39/mo)** — open-source (AGPLv3), real public API (`POST /posts/schedule`), MCP server + `postiz-agent` CLI built for AI pipelines | A hub absorbs LinkedIn's `w_member_social` personal-posting approval gate, which every DIY app hits |
| Newsletter | Beehiiv create-post API + 1-tap send | — |
| Blog/SEO | Ghost Admin API | — |

**Two cautions on the hub:**
1. **Don't self-host Postiz for this.** It drags in Temporal (a workflow engine
   to babysit), you must get your *own* LinkedIn/Google/X apps approved, and
   **LinkedIn personal-profile posting is currently broken on self-hosted**
   (open bug [#1582](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app/issues/1582), Jun
   2026). That's the opposite of autopilot. Use cloud.
2. **Verify before paying** that Postiz cloud posts to your *personal* profile
   via their approved app. If not, the clean fallback is **Ayrshare** (~$149/mo,
   purpose-built API, LinkedIn-personal confirmed, zero OAuth toil).

**Never post a YouTube link into LinkedIn** (~40% reach penalty). Upload a native
LinkedIn video cut; put the YouTube link in the first comment after engagement
starts (native gets ~3.5× the reach).

## 8. Metrics that matter (not vanity)

- **Tier 1 (pipeline proxies, weekly):** profile views from ICP-matching
  titles/companies; demo/contact-page visits from LinkedIn (UTM in comments);
  inbound connection requests from decision-makers; comments that became DMs.
- **Tier 1 trust proxies (the ones unique to this market):** # of named
  consultant endorsements; ETEN/standards listings secured; design partners
  active.
- **Tier 1 movement proxies (Engine 2):** PLG signups attributed to YouTube;
  YouTube tutorial watch-time → signup conversion; ranking for target how-to
  queries; community size/activity.
- **Tier 2 (content quality):** saves + shares/post (LinkedIn); YouTube retention
  (a 12-min video at 65% beats 30% regardless of keywords) + click-through from
  Shorts to long-form; newsletter subscriber growth.
- **Tier 3 (vanity, trend only):** impressions, followers, raw Shorts views.
- **Attribution for a lean team:** a "How did you hear about us?" field on every
  demo form + "had you seen our content before this call?" in discovery. Most of
  LinkedIn's influence is dark-social and won't show in UTMs.

## 10. Risks & honest failure modes

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Confidential leak** (named partner / restricted country) | The triage HOLD gate + keyword blocklist + human review of held segments. Never auto-publish from flagged material. |
| **"AI slop" reputation** in a craft community | Voice library + hook-score gate; founder approval on every piece; specificity over adjectives. Slop here is fatal, not just ineffective. |
| **LinkedIn account ban** | Official-API publishing only; manual DMs/comments; no scraping. |
| **MAST-adjacent perception** | Never claim speed without quality evidence; "Draft Zero" framing; avoid WA association. |
| **Commercial-in-a-mission-ecosystem backlash** | Free-for-the-field; monetize oversight; be explicit you don't profit off community content; lean on ETEN alignment. |
| **Scope creep** (Faithlife's mistake) | Stay in the translation-workflow lane. No church-management, no sermon tools. |
| **Engine rots** (voice drift, model deprecation, API changes) | Monthly: add new approved posts to voice library. Quarterly: revalidate prompts. Buffer absorbs LinkedIn API churn. |

1. **Win the consultants and the standards bodies; LinkedIn is how you

2. **Run your own go-to-market on the same agent infrastructure you sell — your
