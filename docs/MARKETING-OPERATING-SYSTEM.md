# The Aquilla Marketing Operating System

**A fusion of two playbooks — Hormozi (volume / value / omnipresence) and the
Anthropic credibility model (worldview-first / honesty / slow-narrative-fast-
execution) — into one doctrine.** Companion to
[GTM-DISTRIBUTION-PLAN.md](./GTM-DISTRIBUTION-PLAN.md), which it governs.

> The two playbooks are usually presented as opposites. For Aquilla they are
> **mutually immunizing**: each one's fatal failure mode is cured by the other's
> core move. Hormozi-generosity alone reads as a calculated performance →
> Credibility's *radical honesty* cures it. Credibility-worldview alone reads as
> values-washing → Hormozi's *give-it-away-for-real* cures it. This document is
> the operating system that makes that fusion mechanical instead of aspirational.

_Provenance: synthesized then hardened through an 8-agent adversarial workflow
(apply each playbook → bridge analysis → synthesize → 3-lens stress test →
reconcile). The concessions noted inline are the ones the adversarial panel
forced._

---

## 0. The Spine (the one fixed sentence)

> **The next era of Bible translation will be done mostly by the church in its
> own language — and the only responsible way to let that happen is to make
> expert oversight scale with it, not disappear behind it.**

This sentence does not move. It is the slow center both engines orbit. It is
uncomfortable to both camps, and it breaks the moment you put a competitor's logo
on it. Everything below is an instrument for making more of the world believe
this one thing.

**One concession up front (all three critics converged):** lead with *oversight*,
not *democratization*. "Translation by all believers" is semantically adjacent to
the MAST speed thesis, and a consultant hears the headline, not the caveat. The
Reformation/priesthood analogy is now **long-form-only**, used only where the
"experts keep it trustworthy" clause is physically inseparable from the claim. We
never lead a Short, a thumbnail, or a slogan with "democratize."

## 1. Two Cadences Off One Spine

Single worldview, two clocks. We decouple by **tempo, register, and routing —
never by message.**

- **Engine 1 — Trust (slow, prestige, limitation-first).** Consultants, SIL/UBS,
  ETEN/illumiNations, funders. ~Quarterly flagship *sources* (the Draft Zero
  Quality Study, the Sovereignty Covenant, the annual worldview essay). Cold,
  methods-transparent, quotable from a conference stage. Earned media, 1:1,
  LinkedIn long-form, funder briefs.
- **Engine 2 — Movement (fast, omnipresent, free).** MTTs, church teams, PMs,
  students, the Global South. **3–5 long-form/week and 2–3 Shorts/day for the
  first 90 days**, then re-baseline. Weekly newsletter, same-day in-app cards.
  Warm, operator-to-operator, always closing on one frictionless step (try the
  Playground → start a free project → join the community), never "book a demo."

**Conceded — cadence (Hormozi):** one video a week into a near-empty niche is a
hobby, not a land grab. The Showcase harness fans one master into long-form +
LinkedIn cut + Shorts at near-zero marginal cost; a timid cadence on an automated
line wastes the asset. We carpet the lane before anyone notices it's empty.

## 2. The Two Bridge Objects

The first draft made one object (a fidelity teardown) serve both engines. The
panel was right that it can't: fidelity is **risk-removal**, not desire — nobody
in Nairobi searches for a round-trip harness. So we split the seam into two
objects that *cross-derive* each other.

- **Engine-2 magnet — the Draft Zero Playground.** Public, no-login: paste a
  verse, watch Draft Zero appear in your language, in your browser, on a
  Chromebook, in ten seconds. One-click "start your real project" on completion.
  This is the screenshot that travels and the magic moment that creates *want*.
- **Engine-1 anchor — the Open Fidelity Conformance Suite.** Aquilla must **not
  own the scoreboard.** A young vendor publishing a benchmark it designed, on a
  corpus it chose, where the incumbent loses, is a competitive-marketing artifact
  in a robe — pure MAST-reflex fuel. So Aquilla ships the *test*, never the
  comparison table: an unbranded, reproducible conformance suite handed to a
  neutral consultant working group who run it and publish. Aquilla submits its
  own tool like everyone else and publishes its own loss rows — never a table
  with a named competitor in it.

**The fusion is the cross-derivation, not the object.** Each Playground language
run spawns a consultant-facing fidelity note; each conformance run spawns a
90-second "what we found and what it can't do yet" Short. Two objects, one
recurring seam.

## 3. The Mutual-Immunization Rule (instrumented, not postered)

> **Honesty makes the generosity believable; generosity proves the values aren't
> theater.**

The cure is real but **conditional**, and the conditions are mechanical, not
aspirational:

1. **The admission is load-bearing, discoverable, and product-resident — not
   stapled to every hook.** Front-loading "Draft Zero is weaker in low-resource
   languages" onto every Short is conversion suicide *and* reads as a humblebrag.
   The limitation lives **in the product** — a low-resource confidence warning in
   onboarding, in the editor, in the consultant's oversight view, where it costs
   sign-ups and helps the user. Marketing *reports* a product fact rather than
   performing a confession. The rule: the truth must be **impossible to miss
   before commitment**, not present in every video.
2. **Public-feed parity.** Routing is a fiction on YouTube — a consultant
   searches "Aquilla" and sees the same Shorts the MTTs see. So **every**
   field-facing Short passes the same limitation-first, no-speed-without-quality
   gate as a flagship. Test: would this embarrass you screenshotted onto a
   conference slide? If yes, it doesn't ship.
3. **The tripwire is a watched metric.** The day field-side is monetized, or the
   low-resource limitation's appearance-rate in published assets drops below a
   floor, an alert fires. Both immunizations fail together, and worse than if
   neither had been claimed.
4. **Irreversibility is structural, not rhetorical.** "Free-for-the-field-
   forever" is a pricing line a board can revert — so we bind it: a public
   Sovereignty Covenant with a governance clause and a named external
   accountability partner (CARE-principles page, foundation-held commitment). We
   stop *calling* it irreversible until it *is*.

## 4. One Shared Machine (fast lane, split gate, forced cross-derivation)

```
source artifact (transcript OR Showcase walkthrough OR Playground/conformance run)
  → confidentiality + redaction gate   [transcript-derived ONLY]
  → extract insight atoms
  → draft through the RAILS SKILL       ← encoded once, applied to everything
  → fact-library citation check         ← scores / fidelity numbers / pricing-boundary / load-bearing-limitation tag
  → HUMAN APPROVE  (split queue)
  → omnipresent publish (one master fans per channel)
```

- **Split queue.** Pure-UI tutorial and walkthrough content carries no
  confidential atoms — it **skips the redaction gate** and uses **batch
  approval** (a week of Shorts in one 20-minute sitting). Full human-approve is
  reserved for transcript-derived assets and any quality/fidelity-number claim.
- **The rails skill is the instrumented immunization gate.** No asset passes the
  fact-library check unless (a) any capability claim carries a tagged load-bearing
  limitation *or* resolves to the in-product limitation surface, and (b) any
  "free" claim resolves against the canonical pricing-boundary fact.
- **Forced cross-derivation.** Every Engine-1 source MUST emit ≥1 Engine-2
  artifact and vice versa. A Quality Study auto-spawns a "what we found and what
  it can't do yet" Short; a Showcase walkthrough auto-spawns a one-paragraph
  consultant fidelity note. Cross-engine derivation is a **required pipeline
  output**, not an option.
- **The org seam.** One **Doctrine Owner** owns the spine, the rails skill, and
  the fact library, sitting *above* both engines — with an explicit **Engine-2
  fast-lane SLA** (rails check in hours, never quarterly sign-off) so the shared
  gate never becomes Engine 1's veto over Engine 2's tempo.

## 5. Rules of Engagement (what each engine must never borrow)

- **Movement → never run volume tactics at consultants.** No speed hooks, no
  "translate the Bible in minutes," no impression-chasing pointed at Engine 1.
  But because the feed is public, the *floor* for every Short is already the
  consultant-safe standard (§3.2).
- **Establishment → never slow the movement to prestige cadence.** No gating
  field output behind quarterly sign-off or redaction beyond what confidentiality
  strictly requires. Engine 1's prudence at Engine 2's tempo is asphyxiation; the
  fast-lane SLA enforces this.
- **Never let the warm voice touch a flagship**, and never publish a prestige
  artifact on a content-calendar rhythm — both make it un-citable.
- **Precedence rule for contradictory weeks.** When a fidelity bug surfaces and
  Engine 1 wants a slow teardown while Engine 2 wants to keep shipping wow: **the
  spine and the load-bearing-admission gate win over cadence.** Engine 2 *pauses
  the affected wow* until the admission is drafted, rather than racing the
  correction. Honesty beats the calendar — explicitly, as a tiebreaker.

**One rejected objection.** Hormozi says make views/subscriber-velocity *real
targets* for two quarters in the empty niche. **Partially rejected.** We adopt
**search-impression share on "Bible translation tools/process"** as a real
Engine-2 target (that *is* category capture, not vanity). But raw subscriber count
and views stay diagnostic: in *this* market, a churned skeptic minted by attention
you can't yet serve is more expensive than the view was worth.

**The sequencing concession that outranks the cadence debate (Hormozi's strongest
point).** The volume taps open *after* the magic-moment path is flawless. The
product-health memory trail (sync-token 403s loading zero files, back-translation
staleness, heap churn) says paste-verse-to-Draft-Zero must work every time on a
Chromebook *first*. Pouring movement-scale attention onto a leaky product mints
churned skeptics in the one audience whose trust you cannot rebuy. Name it out
loud: the doctrine's earlier timidity was partly the product protecting itself.

**Measure the right thing.** Movement: free projects/week, 24h activation,
weekly-active translating teams, community-join→active, search-impression share.
Establishment: named design partners, citations of the Study/Suite, funder
references to the Covenant. Shared revenue line: oversight-layer conversions and
seats-under-oversight.

## 6. Proof of Fusion — one transcript, one week, both cadences

A Tuesday team-meeting transcript covers a fidelity fix for footnote preservation
in USFM round-trip.

- **Mon:** transcript → confidentiality/redaction gate → insight atoms. One atom:
  "footnote-loss class fixed; verified on conformance corpus."
- **Tue (Engine 1, slow lane):** atom → drafts a **consultant fidelity note** for
  the next conformance-suite run, framed limitation-first ("fixed for inline
  footnotes; cross-reference notes still normalize — here are those rows"). Rails
  check, full human-approve. Becomes a *source*.
- **Wed (forced cross-derivation):** that same source **must** emit an Engine-2
  artifact → a 90-second Short: "We just made your footnotes survive export — and
  here's the one kind that still doesn't yet." The load-bearing limitation rides
  *inside* the wow, not stapled to it. Batch-approved (pure-UI), fast-lane SLA.
- **Thu (Engine 2, fast lane):** Showcase harness records the fix as a walkthrough
  → long-form + LinkedIn cut + a vertical Short. This walkthrough **auto-spawns** a
  one-paragraph consultant note feeding back into Engine 1's next quarterly Suite —
  the loop closes both directions.
- **Fri:** in-app card to active teams: "Footnote round-trip improved." Newsletter
  references the fidelity note (Engine 1) *and* links the Playground (Engine 2).

One molecule. One rails gate. Two clocks, two registers — and a derivation arrow
crossing the aisle in *both* directions inside a single week. That is the fusion.

## The three sentences a busy founder should remember

1. **Fix the ten-second magic moment first, then open the volume taps** —
   movement-scale attention on a leaky paste-verse path mints churned skeptics in
   the one audience you can't rebuy.
2. **Ship the test, never the scoreboard, and put the hard limitation in the
   product, not in every hook** — that's what keeps the give-away from reading as a
   benchmark-in-a-robe and the honesty from reading as a humblebrag.
3. **One Doctrine Owner above both engines, a fast lane so trust never vetoes
   tempo, and a hard rule that every source must derive across the aisle** —
   that's the difference between a true fusion and two plans sharing a font.
