How we price compute

The margin, stated plainly

We resell AI compute at about four times what it costs us, and five times on autonomous agent runs. Here is what that margin buys, and how to route around it if you would rather not pay it.

What the margin covers

A pack buys more than tokens. It covers model routing, retries, caching, the evaluation suite that catches quality regressions before your translators see them, and a person to call when a draft comes back wrong.

There is always another route

Drafting a complete Bible runs to roughly 31,000 cells: about $1,250 of compute at our rates, or about $310 on your own provider key. Institution customers bring their own key. Anyone can self-host and buy compute direct.

Your text stays yours

An unpaid invoice never locks a team out of its own translation. A lapsed plan pauses oversight features and AI drafting, nothing more. The text stays readable and exportable, permanently.

What counts as a cell

A cell is one translation unit: roughly a verse of scripture, or a segment of similar length in other work. Long cells cost more than short ones, and owners can see the exact accounting in Settings → Usage.
