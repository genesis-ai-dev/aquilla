Another way entirely

Run Aquilla yourself

Aquilla's core is open source. An organization with technical staff can run the whole workspace on its own infrastructure, with its own database and its own AI provider, and pay us nothing.

We think you should know that before you talk to us about price. Self-hosting is a real option, and for some institutions — particularly where translation data cannot leave the country — it is the right one.

How we price compute

The part most pricing pages leave out

We resell AI compute at roughly four times what it costs us, and five times on agent runs, which are the ones that compound fastest. We would rather tell you that plainly than have you discover it.

Why there is a margin at all

A pack buys more than tokens: model routing, retries, caching, the evaluation suite that catches quality regressions before you see them, and someone to call when a draft comes back wrong.

Why it is still fair

Because you can always leave it. Bring your own key on Institution, or self-host and buy compute directly. A price with a visible exit is a price we have to keep honest.

What we will not do

We will not lock a team out of its own translation over an unpaid invoice. A lapsed plan loses oversight features and AI drafting. Your text stays yours, readable and exportable, permanently.

What counts as a verse

Allowances are measured in translation units — one verse, or one segment of comparable length in non-scripture work. Long verses cost more than short ones; the exact accounting is visible to your owners in Settings → Usage.
